Common Startup Analysis Pitfalls and Quality Evaluation Criteria

This document outlines common mistakes in startup market analysis and criteria for evaluating analysis quality.

## Marketplace-Specific Pitfalls

### The Cold Start Problem
Two-sided marketplaces face a chicken-and-egg problem: buyers need sellers, sellers need buyers.
Quality check: Does the analysis address how to bootstrap the initial supply/demand?

### Network Effects Misunderstanding
Not all marketplaces have strong network effects. Some have weak or even negative network effects.
Quality check: Does the analysis explain why this specific marketplace would benefit from network effects?

### Unit Economics Trap
Many marketplaces achieve scale but never achieve profitability due to poor unit economics.
Quality check: Does the analysis consider take rate, transaction costs, and contribution margin?

### Winner-Take-All Assumption
Not all markets are winner-take-all. Some markets support multiple players (fragmentation).
Quality check: Does the analysis explain why this market would consolidate vs. fragment?

## Competitive Analysis Quality Criteria

### Depth of Competitive Research
Superficial analysis mentions "Uber" or "Airbnb" without analyzing actual competitors in the target market.
Quality check:
- Are direct competitors identified by name?
- Are indirect competitors and substitutes considered?
- Is competitive differentiation clearly articulated?

### Market Saturation Assessment
Vague statements like "growing market" without evidence or specific saturation metrics.
Quality check:
- Is market saturation quantified (# of competitors, market share distribution)?
- Are recent market entries/exits mentioned?
- Is timing justified (why now vs. 5 years ago)?

### Barriers to Entry
Missing analysis of what prevents new entrants or why existing players haven't solved this.
Quality check:
- What are the technical barriers?
- What are the regulatory barriers?
- What are the capital barriers?
- What are the moat/defensibility factors?

## Brand DNA Analysis Quality Criteria

### Surface-Level Strength Analysis
Listing obvious strengths without explaining HOW they transfer to the new market.
Quality check:
- Does the analysis explain the mechanism of strength transfer?
- Are there counter-examples where this strength didn't transfer?
- Is the "X for Y" analogy justified or forced?

### Missing Weakness Consideration
Only highlighting strengths without considering what the X brand does POORLY.
Quality check:
- What did the X brand struggle with initially?
- What criticisms does the X brand face?
- What would be different in the Y market?

## Market Research Quality Criteria

### TAM Overestimation
Taking entire market size without considering actual addressable segments.
Quality check:
- Is TAM broken down into SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market) and SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)?
- Are realistic penetration rates used?
- Is the beachhead market identified?

### Trend Misinterpretation
Citing macro trends without showing causal link to specific opportunity.
Quality check:
- Is the trend directly related to the opportunity?
- Is the trend durable or temporary?
- Are counter-trends considered?

### Customer Pain Point Validation
Assuming pain points exist without evidence of customer willingness to pay.
Quality check:
- Is there evidence customers currently pay for solutions?
- Is the pain point acute or mild?
- Is the frequency of the pain point sufficient?

## Red Flags Requiring Deeper Investigation

1. **Regulatory Risk Ignored**: Sharing economy ideas often face regulatory challenges
2. **Supply-Side Difficulty**: Labor marketplaces where supply is hard to recruit/retain
3. **Low Frequency Use Case**: Transactions that happen once per year are hard to build engagement
4. **Commoditized Service**: If the service is undifferentiated, race to bottom on price
5. **High CAC Market**: Some markets have inherently high customer acquisition costs
6. **Long Sales Cycle**: Enterprise markets with 12+ month sales cycles require deep pockets

## Evaluation Framework for Analysis Quality

### Excellent Analysis (Approve)
- Specific competitors named and differentiated
- Quantified market metrics provided
- Clear barriers to entry identified
- Unit economics considered
- Go-to-market strategy outlined
- Realistic about challenges

### Weak Analysis (Loop Back)
- Generic statements without specifics
- Missing competitive landscape detail
- No mention of barriers or challenges
- Overly optimistic without evidence
- Analogies that don't transfer
- Missing key market dynamics

### Fatal Flaws (Reject)
- Demonstrably wrong factual claims
- Ignoring obvious major competitors
- Regulatory impossibility
- Market already saturated with failed attempts
- Fundamental business model flaw
